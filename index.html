About DJ2 Capital Research + AI Integration

DJ2 Capital Research is a research portfolio. My research is primarily in where my interests lie: M&A and restructuring. I leverage AI to build valuations, slides and financial analysis. The prompt engineering is my own.

I started this after learning the theory and fundamentals of finance, to apply these concepts to the kind of work I’ll likely see in the industry. Taking an Excel with AI course and having conversations with AI engineers in the fintech space sparked my interest in building this project.

It’s one side of my preparation for investment banking, and more of a passion project. It’s also a way to build skills that carry over: prompt engineering, AI in Excel and PowerPoint, web design, investment analysis and presentation building.

The other side is studying theory through a traditional investment banking recruitment lens.

For clarity: the slides are created through AI research that I prompt, using real, sourced data. The investment recommendations based on the analysis are purely my own.
